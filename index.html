In saturation the pinch-off point creeps toward the source as VDS rises. The channel gets shorter, so ID keeps rising a little instead of staying flat.

ΔVth = γ(√(2φF + VSB) − √2φF)

Reverse-biasing the body widens the depletion layer under the gate. The gate must pull harder to invert the surface, so Vth rises.

ΔVth = −η · VDS, η ∝ 1/L²

Drain-induced barrier lowering: in a short channel the drain's field reaches the source and lowers its barrier, so Vth drops as VDS rises.

VDSsat = VOV·EcL / (VOV + EcL)

Carriers have a top speed. In a short channel the field is strong enough to reach it, so current saturates early (VDSsat < VOV) without a full pinch-off.
